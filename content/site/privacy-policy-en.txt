This Privacy Policy provides information about our practices. It does not replace any separate notice or consent required for a particular activity, such as recording a lesson or using personal information for promotional purposes.

3. COLLECTING YOUR PERSONAL DATA

The information we process depends on the features you use and whether you are an adult learner, a Child Learner or a parent or guardian.

Information You Provide

Account and contact information may include:

• Full name and username or profile identifier.
• Email address and telephone number.
• Age or age range.
• Country of residence.
• The relationship between a parent or guardian and a Child Learner.

Educational information may include:

• Language level and learning objectives.
• Lesson bookings and attendance.
• Homework and other submitted learning materials.
• Learning progress, assessments and teacher feedback.
• Information exchanged during lessons and support requests.

Payment and transaction information may include:

• Purchased packages or subscription details.
• Payment status and transaction references.
• Invoices and related accounting records.

Payment-card details are processed by the relevant payment provider. Hurra Lingo does not store full payment-card details.

Audio and Visual Information

Live lessons involve the transmission of participants’ voices and, when enabled, camera images. We may also process materials shared during a lesson.

Where lessons are recorded, we process the resulting audio and video recordings as described in Section 5. Participating in a live lesson and storing a recording are separate processing activities.

Usage and Technical Information

When you access the Services, we may process information such as:

• IP address.
• Device type and operating system.
• Browser type and version.
• Service access times and activity timestamps.
• Feature interactions and usage information.
• Technical logs and diagnostic information.
• Device or application identifiers used for service operation and diagnostics, subject to applicable restrictions for children.

This information helps us operate the Services, diagnose problems, maintain security and understand how features perform.

Information From Other Sources

We may receive information from teachers, parents or guardians, payment providers and technical providers where necessary to deliver and administer the Services.

4. HOW WE USE YOUR PERSONAL DATA

We use Personal Data for the following purposes:

Providing the Services

To create and manage accounts and learner profiles, arrange lessons, deliver educational materials and provide access to purchased services.

Supporting Learning

To assess language proficiency, track progress, provide teacher feedback and adapt educational support to the learner’s needs.

Communicating With You

To respond to requests and send lesson information, booking updates, account notices and other necessary service communications.

Managing Payments

To confirm payments, administer purchased packages, process refunds and maintain accounting records.

Maintaining Safety and Security

To protect learners and staff, investigate misuse, prevent fraud and maintain the security of accounts and systems.

Improving the Services

To diagnose technical problems, evaluate service quality and improve platform reliability and educational delivery.

Meeting Legal Obligations

To comply with applicable laws, respond to lawful requests and establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We do not sell Personal Data. We do not use children’s learning information or lesson recordings for unrelated advertising or commercial profiling.

5. CAMERA, MICROPHONE AND LESSON RECORDING

Camera and microphone access support participation in live lessons. Where the Application requires device permissions, those permissions are requested before access. You can manage permissions through your device settings, although disabling them may affect the relevant lesson features.

Granting a camera or microphone permission does not itself authorise us to record a lesson or use its contents for another purpose.

Lessons may be recorded for specified purposes such as:

• Educational review and feedback.
• Teaching quality assurance.
• Teacher training.
• Learner safety.
• Investigating complaints or resolving disputes.

Before recording, we provide the relevant information about its purpose and handling and obtain separate consent where required by applicable law.

Access to recordings is limited to authorised personnel and Service Providers who need access for the disclosed purposes. Recordings are subject to the retention and deletion principles in this Privacy Policy.

Accepting our Terms and Conditions does not grant permission to publish a learner’s image, voice or work for advertising. Such promotional use requires separate, specific permission from the appropriate adult learner or parent or guardian.

6. AI-SUPPORTED LEARNING

Hurra Lingo uses AI-supported tools to assist with learning progress analysis, personalised educational recommendations, lesson summaries and platform improvement.

Personal Data used for these features is limited to what is necessary for the relevant purpose. Where an external provider processes information for an AI feature, that processing is subject to appropriate contractual and data protection safeguards.

AI-supported tools operate under human oversight. They do not replace teachers or make solely automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects on learners.

Where a feature requires additional information or consent, we provide this before the relevant processing begins.

Adult learners and parents or guardians may contact hello@hurralingo.com to ask about AI-supported processing and exercise applicable rights concerning personalisation.

7. LEGAL BASES FOR PROCESSING

Where UK or EU data protection law applies, we rely on an appropriate lawful basis for each processing activity. Depending on the activity, this may include:

Performance of a Contract

Processing necessary to provide services under our agreement with an adult account holder, including purchase administration and service delivery.

Legal Obligation

Processing necessary to meet binding legal requirements, including relevant accounting and regulatory duties.

Legitimate Interests

Proportionate processing necessary for interests such as service security, fraud prevention and reliability, after considering the rights and interests of affected individuals, particularly children.

Consent

Processing based on a separate, informed choice where consent is appropriate or required.

A parent’s agreement to purchase lessons does not automatically provide the lawful basis for every use of a child’s information. We consider children’s information separately and obtain parental authorisation where required.

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

10. RETENTION OF YOUR PERSONAL DATA

We retain Personal Data only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected or to meet a lawful retention requirement.

In deciding the retention period, we consider:

• Whether the account or educational relationship remains active.
• Whether information remains necessary to provide lessons, progress reporting or support.
• The specific purpose of a lesson recording and whether it remains necessary for that purpose.
• Applicable accounting, tax and other legal requirements.
• Any relevant safety concern, fraud investigation or legal dispute.
• The sensitivity of the information, especially information about children.

We do not retain lesson recordings indefinitely merely because storage is available.

When information is no longer needed, we delete it securely or irreversibly anonymise it.

Where limited information must be retained after a deletion request, we explain the relevant categories, reasons and applicable period or criteria. Retained information is restricted to the purpose justifying its continued retention.

Backup copies are removed through the applicable backup retention cycle and protected against ordinary use in the meantime.

Requesting Deletion Through Our Website

https://www.hurralingo.com/iletisim/

Requesting Deletion by Email

You may also email hello@hurralingo.com with the subject “Hurra Lingo – Account Deletion”.

Include the email address associated with the account and identify any child profiles included in your request. Do not send your password or full payment-card details.

You do not need to reinstall the Application to submit a request through our website or by email.

We may take proportionate steps to verify account ownership or parental authority. Once verified, we delete the account and associated Personal Data unless limited retention is necessary and lawful. Where relevant, we also instruct Service Providers to delete information processed on our behalf.

We explain the expected handling time and any necessary retention exceptions when processing your request. Temporary suspension or deactivation is not treated as account deletion.

Account deletion, subscription cancellation and refund requests are separate processes. We explain any relevant effect on remaining lessons or billing and provide support with necessary steps.
