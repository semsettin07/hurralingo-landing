About fees

  • 2027 fees are in Turkish lira and exclude VAT.
  • Turkish teacher and native teacher salaries are different. You can choose your teacher in the calculator.
  • 4 per month for 1 lesson per week for monthly membership; There are 8 lessons per month for 2 lessons per week. 6-month packages include 24 / 48 lessons, annual packages include 48 / 96 lessons.
  • A membership discount of 5% is applied for 6-month membership and 10% for annual membership. For cash payment, an additional discount of 5% and 10% is applied to the discounted course fee, respectively. At each step, the lesson fee is rounded up to full lira.
  • There is no separate cash / installment payment option for monthly membership. Installments are paid monthly: 6 installments are applied in the 6-month package and 12 installments are applied in the annual package. The number of installments is calculated by dividing the number of training weeks by 4.
  • Initial fees on the cards are for annual membership and cash payment with a Turkish teacher. Group and mini-group class size and lesson duration reflect current instructional model information.
